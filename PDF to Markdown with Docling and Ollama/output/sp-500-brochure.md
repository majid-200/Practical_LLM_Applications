## Relevance of the S&amp;P 500

The 500 represents approximately 50% of the global equity market capitalization.

Many of the largest exchange-traded funds (ETFs) and mutual funds in the world track The 500. The index supports a large and robust trading ecosystem that includes index futures and options.

With more than 65 years of live index history, The 500 has a long and proud history. Investors analyze The 500's performance and constituents to understand how the U.S. equity market has evolved and reacted to a wide range of historical events.

## Constructing the S&amp;P 500

The 500 is designed to measure the performance of index-eligible stocks listed on selected U.S. exchanges. It is weighted by float-adjusted market capitalization and includes liquidity and tradability criteria in the constituent selection process.

## S&amp;P 500 GICS Sector Weights

The key trend observed in the S&P 500 sector weights is a **decline in the weight of the Information Technology sector** over time. Specifically:  

- **1996**: Information Technology accounted for 33.1% of the S&P 500 weight.  
- **2015**: Its weight dropped to 9.8%.  

This represents a substantial reduction of over 20 percentage points from 1996 to 2015.  

### Additional Trends:  
- **Financials (18.2% → 5.0%)**: A significant decline of 13.2 percentage points, indicating reduced investment focus in financial services.  
- **Healthcare (14.0% → 3.0%)**: A sharp decline of 11.0 percentage points, reflecting a contraction in the healthcare sector.  
- **Consumer Discretionary (9.3% → 7.5%)**: A modest decline of 1.8 percentage points.  
- **Industrials (10.4% → 17.2 לפת)**: A notable increase of 6.8 percentage points, suggesting a shift toward industrial investments.  
- **Consumer Staples (8.6% → 6.0%)**: A slight decline of 2.6 percentage points.  
- **Utilities (3.0% → 2.4%)**: A gradual decline of 0.6 percentage points.  

### Summary:  
While the Information Technology sector’s weight has declined most significantly, **Industrials** and **Consumer Staples** have experienced modest upward shifts. These trends suggest a broader reorientation of the S&P 500 investment focus away from Technology and Healthcare toward Industrial and Consumer staples, indicating a potential shift in investment priorities.

## U.S. Core Equity

The S&amp;P 500 Top 10 Index, S&amp;P 500 Top 50 and S&amp;P 500 Top 20 Select Index and S&amp;P 100 are mega-cap indices that are subsets of the S&amp;P 500.

The S&amp;P Composite 1500 combines the S&amp;P 500 , S&amp;P MidCap 400 ® and S&amp;P SmallCap 600 ® .

The S&amp;P 900 (LargeMid) and S&amp;P 1000 (MidSmall) are combinations of our U.S. size segments.

## Equal Weight

The S&amp;P 500 Equal Weight Index gives each company in the S&amp;P 500 the same weight at each rebalance.

The S&amp;P 500 Equal Weight Futures Index is constructed from the front-quarter E-mini futures contract on equal-weight S&amp;P 500.

## Factors

The S&amp;P 500 single- and multi-factor indices track well-known factors including size, momentum, quality, low volatility and dividends.

## Sectors &amp; Industries

The S&amp;P 500 sector indices follow the Global Industry Classification Standard (GICS) which is a four-tiered industry classification where companies are classified by their principal business activity.

The S&amp;P 500 serves as the basis for the Select Sector™ and S&amp;P 500 Equal Weight Sectors, as well as a variety of capped variants .

## Style

Our style series includes broad, exhaustive style indices, as well as narrower, pure style indices. Style: S&amp;P 500 Growth and S&amp;P 500 Value. Pure Style: S&amp;P 500 Pure Growth and S&amp;P 500 Pure Value .

VIX

Volatility — "The Fear Index": The Cboe Volatility Index (VIX) gives market participants real-time insight into investor sentiment and expected levels of market volatility.

Dispersion — "The Opportunity Index": The Cboe S&amp;P 500 Dispersion Index (DSPX) can be described as a "VIX for dispersion" and measures how differently stocks are performing or are expected to perform.

The S&amp;P 500 is available through a range of sustainability focused methodologies.
